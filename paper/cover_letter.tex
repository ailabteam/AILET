\noindent\textbf{Re: Resubmission of Manuscript AILET-2025-0018}\\
\textbf{``An Empirical Study of Deep Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Scheduling in Satellite QKD Networks''}\\[1em]

\noindent Dear Editors,

Thank you for the opportunity to resubmit our manuscript, and for the constructive
assessments from the Associate Editor and the two reviewers. We were encouraged
that Reviewer 1 recommended Minor Revision and commended the honesty of the
empirical finding, and we have taken the concerns of Reviewer 2 equally seriously.
This new manuscript addresses every point raised. The main improvements are as
follows.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The reward function is now fully specified and the comparison is
  provably fair.} We give the secure-key channel model, the switching-cost
  dynamics, and the complete piecewise reward as explicit equations, and we state
  that every policy (the learned agent and all baselines) is scored by one
  identical physical objective, the total secure key. No policy is evaluated
  against a private or differently weighted reward. This directly resolves the
  central concern of Reviewer 2.

  \item \textbf{We added a switching-cost sensitivity analysis} (the main request
  of Reviewer 1). We retrain a DRL agent for each switching-cost value and show how
  performance, and the learning--heuristic gap, evolve, including a critical cost
  beyond which all policies fail.

  \item \textbf{We added a cost-aware hybrid baseline} (greedy with switching
  hysteresis), tuned by a margin sweep, and explain why it does not beat plain
  greedy in this geometry-dominated setting.

  \item \textbf{We strengthened rigor.} All results are now reported as mean and
  standard deviation over 20 independent seeds, replacing the original single-seed
  numbers. We also corrected a one-step off-by-one in the switching-cost model so
  that a nominal cost of $C$ minutes forfeits exactly $C$ minutes, and retrained
  every model accordingly.

  \item \textbf{We clarified the motivation and scope}, framing the task as a
  network-level scheduling problem, justifying the random lower bound, and adding
  an explicit Limitations discussion on the single-satellite, single-algorithm
  setting and on generalization.
\end{enumerate}

A point-by-point response to every comment accompanies this letter. The full source
code and trained models are available at
\url{https://github.com/ailabteam/AILET}.
